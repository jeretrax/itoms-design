ITOMS Service Management · Massive Landscape

Everything IT could be responsible for, mapped from customer expectation to provider delivery configuration.

This is the ITSM counterpart to the massive GRC landscape. Each capability can exist in the catalog whether or not it is purchased. A customer instance emphasizes what is included, how it is delivered, dependencies, outcomes, and decisions. A provider instance expands the same service into delivery configuration, responsibilities, checklists, monitoring, evidence, escalation, and assurance.
