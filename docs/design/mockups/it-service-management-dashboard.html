Service Lifecycle

1. Selected
Agreement establishes scope.

2. Onboarded
Prerequisites and initial configuration completed.

3. Operational
Recurring responsibilities are active.

4. Assured
Delivery is measured and evidenced.

5. Changed / Offboarded
Scope and responsibility are explicitly closed.
