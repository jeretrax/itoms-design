# ITOMS Vision

**Status:** Proposed  
**Purpose:** Define the enduring product direction for ITOMS.

ITOMS is an IT operating system and operational control plane that connects the full customer lifecycle: discovery, assessment, onboarding, management, support, governance, improvement, and offboarding.

The platform centers work on the customer's operational graph rather than on disconnected tool records. People, companies, locations, assets, identities, applications, services, policies, risks, evidence, costs, and work remain related and understandable in business context.

## Intended outcomes

- Give each role a workspace organized around the work they need to accomplish.
- Maintain one durable operational model across MSP and customer teams.
- Turn observations and technical signals into decisions, accountable work, and measurable outcomes.
- Preserve provenance, history, relationships, and evidence.
- Support SaaS, co-managed, federated, and secure self-hosted operating models.

## Product boundary

ITOMS may coordinate existing PSA, RMM, documentation, security, finance, and cloud platforms before replacing any of their specialized functions. It is not an accounting general ledger and does not expose credentials or unrestricted technician data through customer-facing experiences.
